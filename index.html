About

My history with computers goes back well before PCs — punch cards, line printers, and 300-baud acoustic modems. I’ve been building software and internet applications since before the World Wide Web took shape, and I’m still collecting programming languages the way some people collect refrigerator magnets.

These days you’ll find me writing code alongside AI agents, tinkering with hardware for passion projects, and occasionally staying up until 4:40 in the morning waiting for an agent to finish its work.
